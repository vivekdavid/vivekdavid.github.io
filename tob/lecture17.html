Man and Women: A Gift for Each Other

Lecture 17 - February 6, 1980

In the beginning, men and women hand complementary bodies given as a gift. Man and woman were originally innocent and this innocence facilitated the giving of one another's bodies.

24 That is why a man leaves his father and mother and clings to his wife, and the two of them become one body.* 25The man and his wife were both naked, yet they felt no shame.* - Genesis 2:23 to2 5

Interior innocence - reference to righteous intetion. In the context of sex it means receiving the other person and accepting him or her as a gift.

Opposition to interior innocence reducing the other person to an object (object of lust, object of abuse etc.)

Juxtaposition - the body in proper maritial relationship deepens dignity whereas nakendness outside of this relationship brings shame

Man and woman receiving each other as a gift is god's will. Man and woman receive each other as a gift. The exchange of the gift is mutual and reciprocal. By accepting the woman as a gift, man understands his masculinity better.

Reflections

  • There are traditional reasons for man and woman uniting in exclusive relationships that make sense - it seems to produce better outcome for the kids. It seems to produce more stable and egalitarian social structures. But the pope seems to be going to be going against those reasons for marriage and saying that it's not about these ends but accepting the other person as a gift.
  • It's not really clear what the pope means by accepting the other as a gift and not an ends. In a typical marriage there is some degree of ends - most people's goals are to have children, have a stable partnership etc. Is this wrong? To some extent it does seem like the pope is saying that but it's not really clear of what the alternative giving each other as a gift means, so hopefully he elaborates.
