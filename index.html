100 Acts of love challenge

Show love to others for God. It can be something as simple as a smile, a compliment, helping with a chore. Listening to a family member instead of telling them you are busy. Even something as simple as saying a prayer for someone in private.

Note: I'm not sure whether to just people to do acts of love - or to offer acts of love as an offering to God / think about what the holy spirit would want.





    Acts of love

    desired feedback

    1. Was the site user friendly? Did it feel natural using it and make you want to continue using it, if not what could change?
    2. Did listing acts of love help bring you closer to God, or help bring the people around you to Christ? What could be done to improve the spiritual exercise?
    3. Do you have any tips for making the UI better? (getting rid of the date field instead of inserting today when left blank, adding other fields or relationships?
    4. This is a prototype, the next version will allow you to share your info with friends or with a christian community - do you have any suggestions for stuff you wish to see?
    5. The name 100 acts of love is trademarked (please don't tell them about this) do you have suggestions for a different name. Preferably something catchy that feels like a fun social media challenge.
    6. If you're tech or content person ideas for real world production best way to organize SQL tables(postgres or SQLlite), best way to moderate content, best way to protect privacy of users and person loved (filtering proper names sounds compute intensive, making and enforcing rules is human resource intensive - better approach?)
